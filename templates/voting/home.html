How it works

  1. Register

    Enter your details exactly as they appear on the voter roll and capture your face.

  2. Get accredited

    On election day, enter your registration number and scan your face.

  3. Vote

    Choose one candidate per position. Your face is checked again when you submit.

Your vote is secret

The system records that you voted, so nobody can vote twice, but it never stores who you voted for alongside your identity.
