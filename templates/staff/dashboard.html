Running an election

  1. Upload the voter roll as a CSV or Excel sheet.
  2. Students register with their details and face. Registration closes automatically once voting opens.
  3. Create the election, then add its positions and the candidates for each.
  4. On election day, press Open voting on the election's page, and Close voting when polls close.
  5. Watch live results, then press Publish results to show them to students.
