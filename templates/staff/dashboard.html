Running an election

  1. Upload the voter roll CSV.
  2. Students register with their details and face. Registration closes automatically once voting opens.
  3. Create the election, its positions and candidates in the admin.
  4. On election day, select the election in the admin and run Open voting. Run Close voting when polls close.
  5. Watch live results here; tick Results public after closing to publish them.
